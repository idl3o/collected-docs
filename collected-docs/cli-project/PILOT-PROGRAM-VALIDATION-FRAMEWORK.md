### Risk Assessment and Mitigation

#### Technical Risks
1. **Measurement Sensitivity**
   - Risk: Phenomena too subtle for current instrumentation
   - Mitigation: Multiple measurement approaches, statistical aggregation
   
2. **Implementation Complexity**
   - Risk: Practical deployment challenges
   - Mitigation: Phased implementation, fallback protocols

3. **Environmental Interference**
   - Risk: External factors affecting results
   - Mitigation: Controlled environments, baseline comparisons

#### Ethical and Safety Risks
1. **Consciousness Manipulation Concerns**
   - Risk: Unintended consciousness alteration
   - Mitigation: Informed consent, monitoring protocols, reversal procedures

2. **Privacy and Data Security**
   - Risk: Sensitive consciousness data exposure
   - Mitigation: Encryption, anonymization, access controls

3. **Unintended Consequences**
   - Risk: Unexpected effects from consciousness research
   - Mitigation: Conservative implementation, emergency protocols
