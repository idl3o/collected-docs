# Evolving Second Brain for Obsidian

## Vision

Create an intelligent, self-improving Obsidian plugin that actively researches, enriches, and connects topics of user interest—building a living, ever-evolving network of notes that grows in value and insight with every interaction.

## Core Principles

- **Incremental Wins:** Every improvement, no matter how small, is valuable. The system should propagate and build upon these wins automatically.
- **Continuous Evolution:** The knowledge network is never “done”—it is always learning, refining, and expanding.
- **User-Guided, AI-Accelerated:** The user’s curiosity and intent drive the process; AI augments research, synthesis, and connection.
- **Transparency & Control:** Users can see, understand, and guide what the system is doing at every step.
- **Bidirectional Sync:** Improvements flow both ways—between Obsidian notes and the underlying knowledge graph.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Active Research & Enrichment**
   - The plugin identifies underdeveloped or high-potential notes.
   - It uses AI to research, summarize, and suggest new content or connections.
   - All changes are incremental and reviewable.

2. **Network Growth & Visualization**
   - The system maintains a dynamic graph of notes and their relationships.
   - Users can visualize clusters, gaps, and key concepts.
   - The network structure guides further enrichment and discovery.

3. **Seamless Integration**
   - Works natively within Obsidian, leveraging its markdown and linking features.
   - Syncs improvements between the vault and the knowledge graph (JSON/graphML).

4. **User Feedback Loop**
   - Users can accept, reject, or modify AI suggestions.
   - The system learns from user feedback to improve future recommendations.

5. **Automation with Oversight**
   - Routine enrichment and analysis can be automated (scheduled or on-demand).
   - Users retain ultimate control over what is added or changed.

## North Star Outcomes

- A Second Brain that gets smarter, richer, and more useful every day.
- Users spend less time on manual research and more on creative synthesis and insight.
- The knowledge network becomes a unique, evolving asset—reflecting both the user’s interests and the world’s best information.

## Getting Started

1. **Export your knowledge web** using `export_network.js` to generate JSON, GraphML, and CSV files.
2. **Visualize the network** in Gephi, D3.js, or Obsidian plugins.
3. **Identify gaps** using completeness and degree metrics.
4. **Enrich nodes** with AI-powered scripts, focusing on underdeveloped areas.
5. **Sync improvements** between Obsidian and the knowledge graph.
6. **Iterate and evolve**—the system is designed for continuous, incremental improvement.
