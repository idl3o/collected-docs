# Self-Determination Theory

**Summary:**
Self-Determination Theory (SDT) is a macro-theory of motivation that explains how people's behavior and well-being are regulated by three innate psychological needs: autonomy, competence, and relatedness. The theory proposes that people have a fundamental drive to exercise these needs, which leads to optimal functioning and engagement.
