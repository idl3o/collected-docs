# Metacognition

**Summary:**
Metacognition enables learners to intentionally analyze and refine their cognitive processes, fostering deeper understanding, effective problem-solving, adaptability, and optimized learning. This reflection encompasses recognizing cognitive biases, emotional drivers, and affective responses, enabling learners to develop a more nuanced understanding of their thought patterns and improve their learning strategies.
