# Scientific Method

**Summary:**
The scientific method is a systematic, iterative process of hypothesis formation, experimentation, and validation to develop and refine theories describing natural phenomena. Emphasizing reproducibility, objectivity, and evidence-based conclusions, it allows for the refinement of knowledge through the testing of falsifiable hypotheses.
