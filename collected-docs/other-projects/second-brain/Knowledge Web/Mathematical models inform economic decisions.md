# Mathematical models inform economic decisions

**Summary:**
Mathematical models inform economic decisions by providing a structured framework for evaluating the effectiveness of policy interventions, forecasting market trends, and optimizing resource allocation. This process facilitates data-driven decision making in various industries, from finance to healthcare.

**Key Frameworks:**
- Solving Systems of Linear Equations for Economic Modeling
- Agent-Based Models for Predicting Market Behavior
