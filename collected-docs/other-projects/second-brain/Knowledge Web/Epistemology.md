# Epistemology

**Summary:**
Epistemology delves into the nature of knowledge, exploring how we form and justify beliefs across various domains, emphasizing the interplay between ontology, semantics, and epistemology.

**Key Frameworks:**
- Realism
- Nominalism
- Constructivism
- Empiricism
