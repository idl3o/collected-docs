# Physics (e.g., dynamical systems, fluid dynamics)

**Summary:**
Physics (e.g., dynamical systems, fluid dynamics) is a branch of natural sciences that deals with the study of matter and energy and their interactions. This field involves the exploration of fundamental laws and principles governing natural phenomena, such as gravity, electromagnetism, and quantum mechanics, to name a few.
