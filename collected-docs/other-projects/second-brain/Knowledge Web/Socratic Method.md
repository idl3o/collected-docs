# Socratic Method

**Summary:**
The Socratic Method is a pedagogical approach that harnesses the power of questioning to facilitate critical thinking and promote autonomous learning, empowering students to navigate complex conceptual frameworks through iterative exploration.

**Key Frameworks:**
- Dialectics
- Critical Thinking Frameworks
- Interrogative Inquiry

**Actionable Insights:**
- Fosters meta-cognitive awareness by encouraging self-reflection and evaluation of knowledge claims;
- Promotes epistemological humility by acknowledging the provisional nature of knowledge and the importance of ongoing inquiry;
- Encourages dialogue and collaboration through peer-to-peer questioning, fostering a sense of community and shared responsibility for learning
