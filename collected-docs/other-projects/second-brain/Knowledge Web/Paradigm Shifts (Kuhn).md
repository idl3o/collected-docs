# Paradigm Shifts (Kuhn)

**Summary:**
A paradigm shift in science, as described by Thomas Kuhn, represents a transformative change in the foundation of thought within a scientific discipline, driven by accumulated anomalies that challenge the existing paradigm and are eventually replaced by a novel one, often through a process of crisis and innovation, leading to significant changes in research inquiries, methods, and even the perceived nature of the discipline itself. Key to this shift is the recognition of the limitations of the previous framework, enabling a re-evaluation of fundamental assumptions and the emergence of innovative perspectives.

**Key Frameworks:**
- Normal Science: the persistent puzzle-solving that underlies an accepted paradigm
- Scientific Revolutions: periods of rapid conceptual innovation and radical change
- Incommensurability: the notion that new paradigms may be incompatible with old ones in fundamental ways, leading to conceptual and historical fragmentation

- The extent to which paradigms shape our understanding of reality highlights the importance of critiquing assumptions and questioning fundamental principles

**Connections:**
