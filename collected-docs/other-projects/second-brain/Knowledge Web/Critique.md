# Critique

**Summary:**
A critique facilitates a rigorous examination of an idea, work, or argument, scrutinizing its underlying assumptions and biases to uncover potential flaws and areas for improvement, which can lead to refined understanding, novel insights, and informed decision-making.

**Key Frameworks:**

- Argumentation Theory
- Critical Discourse Analysis
