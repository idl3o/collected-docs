# Social Learning Theory

**Summary:**
Social Learning Theory (SLT) proposes that people learn new behaviors, attitudes, and knowledge by observing and imitating others. This theory, developed by Albert Bandura in the 1960s, suggests that learning can occur through observation, imitation, and reinforcement. SLT emphasizes the role of environmental factors, such as social norms and cultural context, in shaping individual behavior.
