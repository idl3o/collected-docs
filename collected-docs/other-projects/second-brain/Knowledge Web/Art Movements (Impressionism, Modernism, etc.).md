# Art Movements (Impressionism, Modernism, etc.)

**Summary:**
Art movements are transformative periods in the evolution of artistic expression, characterized by distinct stylistic, theoretical, and cultural frameworks that respond to societal, technological, and economic shifts. This summary highlights pivotal art movements from Impressionism to Postmodernism, emphasizing innovative techniques, theoretical approaches, and cultural contexts, as well as the key roles played by avant-garde artists in challenging traditional norms and pushing creative boundaries.
