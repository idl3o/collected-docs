# Argumentation Theory

**Summary:**
Argumentation theory is the study of how individuals construct and maintain arguments to persuade others. It involves the analysis of communicative acts, including claims, reasons, and presumptions, with a focus on social context and audience involvement. This theory provides insights into human decision-making processes and can inform effective communication strategies.
