# Self-Directed Learning

**Summary:**
Self-Directed Learning refers to a process of learning and development where individuals take ownership of their learning journey, making choices about what, when, and how they learn. This approach emphasizes autonomy, self-motivation, and metacognition, allowing learners to adapt to changing contexts and pursue personalized goals.

**Actionable Insights:**
- Self-Directed Learning is not about being self-sufficient, but rather about being able to recognize and leverage opportunities for growth.
- When learners take ownership of their learning journey, they develop a stronger sense of responsibility and motivation.
- Developing metacognitive skills enables learners to reflect on their own learning process and adjust their strategies accordingly.
