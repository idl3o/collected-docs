# Social Learning Framework

**Summary:**
The Social Learning Framework is a theoretical framework that posits that humans learn through observing and imitating others, as well as experiencing environmental conditions. It suggests that social factors, such as culture, family, and reinforcement play a significant role in shaping individual behaviors and attitudes.
