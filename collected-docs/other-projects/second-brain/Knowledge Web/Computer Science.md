# Computer Science

**Summary:**
Computer science encompasses a broad range of technologies and techniques involving the study, design, development, testing, and maintenance of computer systems and software. It involves algorithms, programming languages, computer architecture, artificial intelligence, data structures, databases, networking, machine learning, human-computer interaction, and more.
