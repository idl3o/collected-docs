# Data Science (e.g., predictive analytics, machine learning)

**Summary:**
Data Science is the extraction of valuable knowledge from large datasets using a wide range of techniques, including predictive analytics and machine learning. It involves applying statistical methods to uncover hidden patterns and relationships in data, often combining domain expertise with computational power to drive business decisions.
