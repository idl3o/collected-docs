# Reflection

**Summary:**
Reflection is a deliberate process of distilling lessons from experiences, integrating diverse perspectives, and fostering emotional intelligence. By examining personal biases, emotions, and values, individuals can develop more effective coping strategies, re-evaluate priorities, and cultivate resilience.

- Socratic Method
