# Environmental Ethics (sustainability)

**Summary:**
Environmental ethics refers to the application of moral principles and values to environmental decision-making, aiming to promote sustainable development and preserve the natural world for future generations.

**Key Frameworks:**
- Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
