# Personal Knowledge Management (PKM)

**Summary:**
Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) refers to the process of actively constructing and maintaining a personal knowledge framework, enabling individuals to filter, prioritize, and integrate diverse information sources, ultimately fostering innovative thinking, strategic decision-making, and creative problem-solving.
