# Intuitionistic Logic

**Summary:**
Intuitionistic Logic is a subfield of mathematical logic that deals with the study of intuitionistic propositional and predicate logics. It was developed by mathematician L.E.J. Brouwer in the early 20th century as an alternative to classical logic, focusing on the concept of intuition and the limitations of human knowledge.

**Key Frameworks:**
- Intuitionistic Propositional Logic
- Intuitionistic Predicate Logic
