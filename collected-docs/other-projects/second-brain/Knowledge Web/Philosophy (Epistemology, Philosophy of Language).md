# Philosophy (Epistemology, Philosophy of Language)

**Summary:**
Philosophy of Epistemology and Language explores the nature, limits, and validity of knowledge and its relationship to language. It questions how we can know something, what is considered knowledge, and how words and symbols convey meaning.

**Key Frameworks:**
- Descriptive vs. Prescriptive Linguistics
- Syntax- Semantics Interface
- Pragmatic Ambiguity
